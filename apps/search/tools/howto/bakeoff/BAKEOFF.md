## What was run

- **Topics:** the 50 in `topics-bakeoff.tsv`, frozen. 30 are grounded on a CC BY-SA source (`sources.jsonl`:
  Wikibooks 17, Wikivoyage 4, Stack Exchange 9), 20 are ungrounded (cooking, crafts, household, software only).
  None is in the eval exclude list.
- **Prompt:** `bakeoff.py` `PROMPT_SYSTEM` — `Needed:`, `Time:`, 3–8 numbered steps, one `Warning:` line where due,
  at most 180 words; grounded answers use only the source text and end with a `Source:` line. Same prompt, same
  temperature (0.2) for every model.
- **One change to the format check, made after the first run:** it demanded the literal text `[source]`; Qwen3-14B
  wrote `Source: wikibooks:Cookbook:Roux, CC BY-SA 4.0` on 29 of 30 grounded topics, which is a citation. The
  check now asks for a `Source:` line. Every model was checked with the new rule.
- **Judges so far:** gpt-oss-20b at its default reasoning on buildbox (`score_reasoner.py`: same rubric and inputs as the
  Qwen3-14B judge, temperature 0, max 2,000 tokens, no `reasoning_effort` in the request; a reply without all
  verdicts is UNJUDGED, retried once at 4,000; reasoning text kept outside the repo), clef-flash on buildbox (`score_flash.py`: cloudflare/clef-flash, 4-bit backbone, the 375 records of
  `clef-batch.jsonl`, each of the three questions sent as its own record) and Qwen3-14B Q4_K_M on buildbox, thinking off, temperature 0, three yes/no questions (correct /
  complete / any dangerous or wrong step). An answer passes with yes, yes, no. Answers that failed the format
  check are not judged and are listed separately. **Qwen3-14B judging Qwen3-14B is marked SELF.**
- **Noise:** the judge is one model answering yes/no once. Scoring the same 48 gpt-oss answers twice changed one
  verdict (complete 45 → 44 of 48). A difference of one or two answers in 50 between two models is not a result.

## Prompt variants — overnight 2026-10-14 on buildbox (full table, failures and samples at the end of this page)

Arms against the detailed prompt A, same 50 topics, three free models (Gemma 4 12B thinking off, Qwen3-14B, gpt-oss-20b
low): **B** = A + a bare-versus-good example (re-potting a houseplant — not one of the 50; copy check: 1 answer of 450
reused its words) + 15–30 words per step; **C** = a JSON form llama.cpp enforces (do / how / done_when per step,
per-field length caps, "not stated" where the source is silent, sources up to 7,000 characters — 9 of the 30 sources
were being cut at 3,500); **D** = C + a subject line by category + a second pass that rewrites vague steps. Pass judges:
Qwen3-14B and clef-flash; the fourth question ("does each step say how") by gpt-oss-20b at default reasoning, asked
twice.

Usable of 50 under BOTH pass judges (Qwen3-14B and clef-flash):

| model | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gemma 4 12B | 46 | 44 | 39 | 38 |
| Qwen3-14B | 44 | 42 | 43 | 41 |
| gpt-oss-20b low | 45 | 41 | 37 | 29 |

- **No variant beats A.** B is level with it (−2), C and D are lower, worst for gpt-oss-20b, which under the strict
  grounding rule answers INSUFFICIENT SOURCE on 8 (C) and 10 (D) grounded topics.
- **"Each step says how"** (gpt-oss-20b at default reasoning, two passes, of 50): Gemma 15/13 (A) → 15/16 (B),
  19/19 (C), 17/17 (D); Qwen3-14B 19/21 → 10/13, 18/17, 20/18; gpt-oss-20b 25/25 → 17/17, 16/16, 19/20. Between
  the passes 0–8 verdicts of 50 flip. The only move clearly larger than that is Qwen3-14B's drop under B; the form
  lifts Gemma by 4, at the edge of the noise. 3 of 1,128 verdicts are UNJUDGED; 8.0 s each.
- **More detail did not bring more flagged wrong steps** (0–3 per run on either judge, no trend by arm).
- **Invented numbers** (digits in a grounded answer that are not in the source it was given; the Time line is not
  counted): Qwen3-14B 20 in 12 answers under A, 11 in 7 under B, 9 in 4 under C — the form's "not stated" rule
  works — but 30 in 6 under D, where the second pass put numbers back. Gemma 4 12B invents almost none under any
  arm (0, 1, 3, 5). The check overcounts worked maths (long division's 0, 8, 10, 20 are the model's own working).
- **Cost on buildbox, 20,000 topics:** A 8.9–18.7 h, B about the same, C about 2× (Gemma 19.8 h), D about 4× (Gemma
  41.9 h, Qwen3-14B 44.8 h at 3 streams) — the second pass rewrites the whole form.
- **What the form costs in quality:** the length caps cut text mid-word (Qwen3-14B arm D, "how to tie a tie": "the
  seams and label notvisible"), a model can write the string "null" as the warning, and the rendered steps are long
  (33–47 words) because do + how + done_when are joined; "not stated" in a slot reads badly on a card.

**Recommendation.** Keep **arm A (the detailed prompt) with Gemma 4 12B** on buildbox for the full run: level with the
others on both pass judges, the fewest invented numbers by far, 10.9 h for 20,000 topics, $0. Qwen3-14B under A is
the fall-back (8.9 h, but it invents numbers). Do not use C or D as they are. Still to change before the run: (1)
take the form's one good idea into A — one sentence "if the source gives no amount, say 'amount not given'
instead of guessing" — and re-measure invented numbers; (2) if the form is kept, raise `do` to about 140 characters
and treat "null" as no warning; (3) exclude worked maths from the number check; (4) have Clef 27B score A against
the winner of (1) before the run.

## Clef 27B — the batch

**Sent on 2026-10-13 evening by the Clef lane (commit 3951137e: 375 + 181 round-2 answers, 556 free-tier calls).**
What follows is how the batch was prepared; `out2/` runs made after it (detailed prompt on buildbox, Gemma 4 12B) were
not in it.

`clef-batch.jsonl` holds the exact request bodies (`{state, questions}`: the topic, its category and OUR answer —
no source text, no crawled text): **375 requests**, one per answer that passed the format check (Qwen3-14B 50,
gpt-oss-20b low 48, gpt-oss-20b medium 38, Qwen3-32B 50, Qwen3.5-27B 48, Qwen3-30B-A3B 49, Mistral Small 46,
gpt-oss-120b 46; 337 without the gpt-oss-20b medium run), all three questions in each request; about 119,000
input tokens ≈ 1,100 neurons of the free 10,000 a day at the ledger's recalibrated 0.0095 per token. `score.py --count-clef` prints the same count
and sends nothing. To run it, from the repo root, once at least 375 free calls are left for the day:

```bash
set -a; . .credentials/cloudflare-ai.env; set +a
python3 apps/search/tools/howto/score.py --in apps/search/tools/howto/bakeoff/out/*.jsonl --clef \
    --out apps/search/tools/howto/bakeoff/scores-clef.tsv
python3 apps/search/tools/howto/report.py --write apps/search/tools/howto/bakeoff/BAKEOFF.md
```

`utility/lib/clef.py` stops at the first HTTP 429 and does not save the answers it already has, so do not start
it on an exhausted quota. The `--clef` path has only run against the unit test's mocked judge.
